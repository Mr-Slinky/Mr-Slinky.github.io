Xhuma

IT Anywhere · 2025 to present

Xhuma is the reporting platform I build for IT Anywhere, a managed IT services provider. Each month it gives every client a report on the health and security of their IT.

  1. 8 data sources Monitoring, antivirus, backups, helpdesk
  2. 1 database SQL Server, refreshed every 4 hours
  3. Client reports PDF and Excel, every month

I am the only developer on Xhuma, and I made each of its 20 recorded design decisions. Version 1 runs in production. Version 2, a ground-up rebuild of its core, has been under way since May 2026.
